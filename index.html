SEHAR

Not every beautiful story is written in books. Some are written in the eyes, the smile, the silence, and the existence of someone.

"Some people are born into this world.
Some arrive and give the world a reason to become poetry."

Dear Sehar,

I don't know whether the world has ever told you how beautiful you are in enough words. So I decided to create a world where every little thing about you could have its own place, its own story, and its own poetry.

These pages are not an attempt to define your beauty. They are simply my attempt to appreciate what words have never been able to explain.

Every photograph is a memory. Every poem is an observation. Every line is a small tribute to you.

If one day you read these and feel, even for a moment, that someone truly saw you — then this universe was worth building.

With love, for Sehar. — Eman Zahid
